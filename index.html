Origami for Kids

Fold-along paper crafts with true-size A4 sheets. Ages 6–12.
YouTube: @Kids.JoinDev · playlist Origami for Kids (coming soon) · kids.join.dev

How to print true-size A4: open a project’s templates.pdf → print at 100% / Actual size → do not “fit to page” → measure the black 100 mm bar with a ruler. Default square is 150 × 150 mm.
Symbol key: dashed blue = valley (toward you) · dash-dot red = mountain (away) · ↻ turn over · → fold arrow · number badges = step order (an algorithm!)

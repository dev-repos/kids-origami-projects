#04 Jumping Frog

Ages 7+ · ~15 min · Stored energy + a trigger

A paper frog that really jumps. Fold a zig-zag spring, press the back… boing!

Video: watch on YouTube (Origami for Kids · @Kids.JoinDev)

Print tip: open a PDF → print at 100% / Actual size → do not “fit to page” → measure the black 100 mm bar with a ruler. Fold sheet = print both sides, flip on the long edge, then cut out the square.
